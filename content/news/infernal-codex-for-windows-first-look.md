Infernal Codex for Windows is a table companion for running a D&D 5e game over your own Wi-Fi. The DM runs it on a computer at the table, players join from their phones, and a TV can show the shared map. It is separate from the Android app, and it is **coming soon: there is no download yet.**

The video above is a first look, recorded in a pre-release build with made-up sample campaign data.

## What the video shows

- **Prep that remembers.** Link NPCs, places and sessions, and every mention shows up on the card it points to, with a relationship graph across the campaign.
- **Optional AI prep help.** A button that suggests an NPC's name and description. It is off unless you add your own Claude API key, and when you use it the request goes to Anthropic over the internet.
- **Players join from their phones.** Scan a QR code or type a PIN on the same Wi-Fi, and the DM approves each player.
- **Hidden until you say so.** The DM sees staged monsters and fog that the players' screens do not, until the DM reveals them. Monster hit points stay on the DM's screen.
- **Combat from the phone.** Initiative, your turn, Rage, moving your token, attack and damage rolls, and spells with their slots.
- **Sessions become history.** Log a live session to your campaign when it ends.
